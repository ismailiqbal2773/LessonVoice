# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 29 October 2026
- Strictly offline OmniVoice neural English/Urdu learning packs with four instruction/seed voice characters and a fixed-text preview. No robotic or cloud fallback.
- Isolated Python 3.12 environment, pinned CPU dependencies and a one-time integrity-checked download of model revision c5fdb5ccb189668d56333f77ba2629f4cd7535f4.
- Editable sections, pronunciation replacements, teacher questions, transcripts and WAV/ZIP export.
- Draft preservation, stale-audio checks and accessible responsive workflow explanation.
- Input/host/origin bounds, plain-text speech handling and validated audio exports.
- Separate noncommercial model-use designation and tokenizer-license notices; weights are not redistributed. The early eSpeak feasibility prototype is superseded, not a runtime fallback. Actual verification and remaining limits are listed in docs/VALIDATION.md.
